## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a square finished logo image for a Minecraft mod TACZ: Art of Sniping. Input image is the reference for the existing TACZ ZERO wordmark: closely preserve its distinctive white TAC typography above the silver ZERO typography, its turquoise segmented circular scope emblem at the left (the emblem supplies the stylized Z identity). Retain the recognizable complete reference wordmark as a single grouped element in the upper middle of the square, cleanly reconstructing its tiny low-resolution lettering. Underneath that wordmark add exactly "AoS", centered, large and clearly legible, uppercase A, lowercase o, uppercase S, in a matching bold futuristic geometric sans-serif typeface with the same clean silver/white finish and subtle turquoise accents. Exact visible text consists of the reference TAC ZERO lettering plus the new AoS line; do not duplicate or invent additional text. Dark charcoal / desaturated blue-grey background similar to the reference, very subtle texture and soft teal ambient shading, understated and uncluttered. Flat straight-on graphic, sharp edges, no physical product mockup, no border, no guns or characters, no extra emblems, no watermark. Balanced compact square composition with comfortable margins and good readability as a small mod-list icon. Opaque background. Output one square logo.
